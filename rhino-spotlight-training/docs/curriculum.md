# Curriculum — Followspot Operation (PROPOSED)

Audience: stagehands with general crew experience and no followspot experience.
Format: short modules (90 s – 4 min), each focused on one skill, so lessons can be
re-shot individually and watched on a phone during a break.

Status: the outline is a proposal. Module content beyond Lesson 01 is not scripted.
Items marked † depend on Rhino's equipment list or an instructor's procedures and
cannot be scripted from generic sources.

## Unit 1 — Foundations

| # | Lesson | Target length | Learning outcome | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Introduction to Followspot Operation (pilot)** | 90 s | Can describe what a followspot does, where it's run from, its core controls, and how cues reach the operator | Storyboarded |
| 02 | Followspot Anatomy in Detail | 3 min | Can identify and describe each control, including chopper/trim and fade controls | Generic first; model-specific variants † |
| 03 | Light Sources and What They Mean for You | 2 min | Knows the operational differences between arc/discharge and LED sources (warm-up, dowser behavior, heat) | Hazard content needs instructor review |
| 04 | The Spot Position | 2–3 min | Knows the kinds of positions and what to check on arrival | Working-at-height content † |

## Unit 2 — Communication

| # | Lesson | Target length | Learning outcome | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 05 | Headsets and Comms Etiquette | 2 min | Can use a headset channel properly, knows when to talk | Comms systems vary |
| 06 | Anatomy of a Spot Cue | 3 min | Can interpret standby / pick-up / size / color / go / fade-out calls | Calling conventions vary by caller and show † |

## Unit 3 — Technique

| # | Lesson | Target length | Learning outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07 | Aiming with the Sight | 2 min | Can aim before opening the dowser |
| 08 | Pick-ups and Fade-outs | 3 min | Can execute a clean pick-up and fade |
| 09 | Sizing the Shot | 2 min | Knows common shot sizes and how iris/zoom produce them |
| 10 | Tracking Movement | 3 min | Can follow a moving performer smoothly; leads vs. lags |
| 11 | Color Changes | 2 min | Can make clean color changes, in and out of light |

## Unit 4 — Show Readiness

## Assessment (later)

A short practical checklist per unit for supervisors to sign off in person. Video alone
does not qualify anyone to operate equipment; the course should say so.
